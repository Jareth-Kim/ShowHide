Deadlock Trivia

Look, I really like the game.

Which character is the only one to be immune to Nurse Harrow's fear status effect from her ult?

Warden. After Nurse Harrow was added in the City Never Sleeps major update, they gave Warden an immunity to fear during his ult, due to the fact that he has multple voicelines where he claims to never show fear.

At what time do the first Bridge Buffs spawn on the map?

They spawn in at 5 minutes into a match.

What were the original colors of the 4 lanes, when Deadlock was first made public?

Yellow, Orange, Blue, and Purple. Yellow lane was called York, Orange was called Orchard, Blue was called Broadway, and Purple was called Park.

Who was the first character added after Deadlock lifted the NDA and became public?

Mirage was added on September 26th, 2024, after the NDA was lifted on August 24th that same year.
